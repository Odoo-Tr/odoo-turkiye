Turkish Banks

Adds the 67 banks operating in Türkiye with their EFT codes and BIC (SWIFT) codes. When a Turkish IBAN is entered on a contact, the bank is picked automatically.

TR62 0001 0012 3456 7890 1234 56: digits 5 to 9 of a Turkish IBAN are the bank code, 00010 = T.C. Ziraat Bankası A.Ş.

Features

  • 35 deposit banks, 21 development and investment banks, 10 participation banks and 1 bank under the Savings Deposit Insurance Fund.
  • Official names from the BDDK bank list; EFT code on the bank form, searchable in the list.
  • On a bank account, the bank is filled in from a Turkish IBAN when it is empty; a bank chosen by hand is kept.
  • BIC codes that could not be verified in a reliable source are left empty (7 banks); one bank without a published EFT code is added with its name and BIC only.

How to use

  1. Open a contact, Accounting > Bank Accounts.
  2. Enter the Turkish IBAN: the bank is selected.

Sources: BDDK (Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency) bank list; EFT and BIC codes from the Banks Association of Türkiye and the CBRT FAST participant list, cross-checked; IBAN structure from the CBRT IBAN communiqué. Banks merge and close; tell us about any missing or outdated record.

Compatibility and support

Odoo 15.0 (tested on Community). Depends only on base. No external service is used. Licensed under LGPL-3. Questions and bug reports: destek@odoo-tr.com
