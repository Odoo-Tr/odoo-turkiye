TR FX Revaluation

Revalues open foreign currency receivables, payables and foreign currency bank accounts at the rate of the revaluation date and books the difference to the exchange gain or loss account (646 / 656), as required by article 280 of the Turkish Tax Procedure Law at the end of each provisional tax period and year.

An open receivable of $10,000.00 booked at 41.20, rate 42.10 on the revaluation date: 9,000.00 TRY exchange gain (debit 120, credit 646).

Features

  • Open receivable and payable items in foreign currency, grouped by account, partner and currency.
  • Items paid after the revaluation date are revalued with the amount still open on that date.
  • Bank and cash journals in a foreign currency are revalued on their balance at the date.
  • Uses the company rate valid in Odoo on the date; our TCMB Exchange Rates module fetches CBRT rates.
  • No VAT and no invoice: revaluation differences are not invoiced.
  • Optional reversal on the next day, so that open items return to their invoice rates.

How to use

  1. Accounting > Accounting > Foreign Currency Revaluation.
  2. Choose the date (default: end of last month) and the journal.
  3. Choose what to revalue and click Revalue: a posted journal entry is created.

The law values foreign currency receivables, payables and deposits at the CBRT buying rate and cash at the banknote buying rate; the module uses the company rate for every item, so adjust cash entries by hand if needed. Only accounts of type receivable or payable and foreign currency bank and cash journals are revalued. Check the bulletin date and your period-end entries with your accountant.

Türkçe açıklama

Dönem sonunda açık dövizli alacak, borç ve döviz banka hesaplarını değerleme günündeki kurla yeniden değerler, farkı 646 / 656 hesabına yazar (VUK 280).

  • Tarihten sonra yapılan tahsilat ve ödemeler hesaba katılmaz; kalem o gün açık olduğu tutarla değerlenir.
  • Döviz banka ve kasa defterlerinin hesapları da değerlenir.
  • Değerleme farkı faturalanmaz, KDV hesaplanmaz. İstenirse ertesi gün ters kayıt atılır.
  • Kasadaki efektif için efektif alış kuru gerekir; modül şirket kurunu kullanır.
