TR Financial Statements

Prints the trial balance (mizan), the detailed balance sheet and the detailed income statement in the order of the Turkish uniform chart of accounts (Tek Düzen Hesap Planı), as PDF and Excel. Odoo Community has no financial reports of its own; this module adds the three statements Turkish accountants and tax returns need.

Balance sheet on 31.12.2026: I. Dönen Varlıklar with A. Hazır Değerler, 1. Kasa, 2. Alınan Çekler... and contra accounts such as 257 Birikmiş Amortismanlar (-) shown in parentheses.

Features

  • Trial balance: opening, period and total debit and credit, debit and credit balance per main account (100, 120...), optionally with sub accounts. Income statement accounts start from the fiscal year.
  • Balance sheet: sections I to V, groups A, B, C and numbered lines as in the official form, with the previous year beside it.
  • Income statement: A. Brüt Satışlar to Dönem Net Kârı veya Zararı, with all subtotals, compared with the same period of the previous year.
  • Without closing entries the result of the year is shown as the net profit of the period, and earlier years as retained earnings, so the balance sheet always balances.
  • Closing entries (690 / 692) are left out of the income statement, so it can be printed after the year is closed.
  • Cost accounts not yet transferred (7/A groups 71-78, 7/B 79) are shown on their own lines in the income statement.
  • Posted entries only, or with draft entries. Amounts in the company currency, Turkish number format.

How to use

  1. Accounting > Reporting > Turkish Statements.
  2. Choose Trial Balance, Balance Sheet or Income Statement.
  3. Set the dates and click Print PDF or Export to Excel.

Accounts are classified by the first three digits of their code, so the chart of accounts has to follow the Turkish uniform chart (Odoo's Turkish chart does). Balances of accounts outside it, of memo accounts (class 9) and of accounts with no place in the forms are listed below the statement. Line order and names follow the Tek Düzen Hesap Planı; have your accountant review the statements before filing.

Compatibility and support

Odoo 18.0 (tested on Community). Depends only on account. No external service is used. Licensed under LGPL-3. Questions and bug reports: destek@odoo-tr.com

Türkçe açıklama

Mizan, ayrıntılı bilanço ve ayrıntılı gelir tablosunu Tek Düzen Hesap Planı sıralamasıyla PDF ve Excel olarak verir.

  • Mizan: devir, dönem ve toplam borç/alacak, borç ve alacak bakiyesi; ana hesap ya da alt hesap düzeyinde.
  • Bilanço: I-V bölümleri, A, B, C grupları ve numaralı satırlar; önceki yıl sütunuyla.
  • Gelir tablosu: Brüt Satışlar'dan Dönem Net Kârı'na tüm ara toplamlar; geçen yılın aynı dönemiyle karşılaştırmalı.
  • Kapanış fişi kesilmemişse dönem sonucu 59 grubunda, önceki yılların sonucu 57/58 grubunda gösterilir; kapanış fişleri (690/692) gelir tablosuna alınmaz.
  • Yansıtılmamış 7/A ve 7/B maliyet hesapları gelir tablosunda ayrı satırda görünür.
  • Muhasebe > Raporlama > Turkish Statements.
