Turkish Public Holidays

Loads the national, public and religious holidays of Türkiye into Time Off as public holidays, so they are not counted as working days in leave requests and working calendars.

2026: Ramazan Bayramı from 19 March 13:00 to 22 March, Kurban Bayramı from 26 May 13:00 to 30 May.

Features

  • New Year, 23 April, 1 May, 19 May, 15 July and 30 August as full days.
  • Republic Day: 28 October from 13:00 (half day) and 29 October.
  • Ramazan Bayramı (3.5 days) and Kurban Bayramı (4.5 days) from 13:00 on the eve, for 2025 to 2030.
  • Loaded for Turkish companies on install, from the current year on.
  • A wizard loads other years or companies; existing public holidays are skipped, so running it again creates no duplicates.
  • Times in the Europe/Istanbul time zone. When a religious holiday overlaps a national one (2027, 2029) they are merged into one record, as Odoo does not allow overlapping public holidays.

How to use

  1. Install the module: the holidays of Turkish companies are loaded.
  2. For other years or companies: Time Off > Configuration > Turkish Public Holidays.
  3. Choose the years and companies and click Load Holidays.

Sources: Law no. 2429 on national holidays and public holidays (mevzuat.gov.tr) and the Diyanet calendar of religious days. Diyanet publishes future dates by calculation; compare them with the official calendar as the year approaches. Additional administrative leave days declared for the public sector are not loaded.

Compatibility and support

Odoo 18.0 (tested on Community). Depends only on hr_holidays. No external service is used. Licensed under LGPL-3. Questions and bug reports: destek@odoo-tr.com

Türkçe açıklama

Türkiye'nin ulusal bayramlarını, genel tatillerini ve dini bayramlarını Odoo İzin'e resmi tatil olarak yükler.

  • 1 Ocak, 23 Nisan, 1 Mayıs, 19 Mayıs, 15 Temmuz ve 30 Ağustos tam gün; 28 Ekim 13:00'ten itibaren yarım gün, 29 Ekim tam gün.
  • Ramazan Bayramı 3,5 gün, Kurban Bayramı 4,5 gün; arife 13:00'te başlar. 2025-2030 tarihleri hazırdır.
  • Kurulumda Türkiye şirketleri için yüklenir; başka yıllar için İzin > Yapılandırma > Türkiye Resmi Tatilleri.
  • Kaynaklar: 2429 sayılı Kanun ve Diyanet dini günler takvimi.
