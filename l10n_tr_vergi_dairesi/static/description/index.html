Turkish Tax Offices

Adds the 1,046 Turkish tax offices and revenue offices by province. Contacts and companies get a Tax Office field chosen from a list instead of free text.

For a customer in İstanbul the list shows the 64 tax offices of İstanbul: Kadıköy Vergi Dairesi Müdürlüğü, Beyoğlu Vergi Dairesi Müdürlüğü...

Features

  • Tax Office field below the tax ID on Turkish contacts and on the company.
  • The list is limited to the contact's province when it is set.
  • The tax office is added under the name when Odoo shows a contact with its address.
  • The list can be viewed and edited in Contacts > Configuration > Localization > Tax Offices.
  • Same model and field names as Odoo 20's Turkish localization, which ships this list itself.
  • On Odoo 18 and 19 the Nilvera e-invoice extension (l10n_tr_nilvera_einvoice_extended) has its own tax offices; the two modules cannot be installed together.

How to use

  1. Open a contact located in Türkiye.
  2. Pick the Tax Office below the tax ID.
  3. For your company: Settings > Companies.

The list of tax offices and the structure of the model come from Odoo S.A.'s Turkish localization of Odoo 20 (l10n_tr, LGPL-3), adapted to earlier versions. The list changes over time; the Turkish Revenue Administration is the reference.

Compatibility and support

Odoo 19.0 (tested on Community). Depends only on contacts. No external service is used. Licensed under LGPL-3. Questions and bug reports: destek@odoo-tr.com
