Turkish Annual Leave

Computes the annual paid leave of each employee from seniority under Turkish Labour Law no. 4857, article 53, and allocates it automatically when a service year is completed.

Hired on 1 March 2019, aged 48: on 1 March 2026 the employee completes the 7th service year and gets 20 days.

Features

  • 1 to 5 years (5 included): 14 days. More than 5 and less than 15 years: 20 days. 15 years or more: 26 days.
  • Employees aged 18 or younger, or 50 or older, get at least 20 days; the age is taken on the anniversary.
  • Underground work adds 4 days.
  • Seniority date and underground flag on the employee; the form shows completed years and the entitlement.
  • A daily scheduled action allocates and approves the leave of the service year just completed, once per year.
  • An Allocate button on the employee form does it right away.

How to use

  1. On install, Odoo's Paid Time Off type is flagged as Turkish annual leave (the flag can be moved to another type).
  2. Enter the Seniority Date on the employee form.
  3. Allocations are created on each service anniversary.

These are the legal minimums; contracts may give more. Only the current service year is allocated: leave left from earlier years has to be entered by the HR team, who know the days already taken. Holidays falling within the leave are not counted (article 56) through Odoo's working calendar; see our Turkish Public Holidays module.

On Odoo 20, where time off types became work entry types, the Turkish Paid Time Off type is used.

Compatibility and support

Odoo 20.0 (tested on Community). Depends only on hr_holidays. No external service is used. Licensed under LGPL-3. Questions and bug reports: destek@odoo-tr.com

Türkçe açıklama

4857 sayılı İş Kanunu'nun 53. maddesine göre yıllık ücretli izin hakkını kıdemden hesaplar ve her hizmet yılı dolduğunda izin tahsisini kendiliğinden ekler.

  • 1-5 yıl: 14 gün; 5-15 yıl: 20 gün; 15 yıl ve üzeri: 26 gün.
  • 18 ve daha küçük ya da 50 ve daha büyük yaştaki çalışanlara en az 20 gün; yer altı işlerinde 4 gün fazla.
  • Çalışan formunda Kıdem Başlangıcı girilir; günlük görev tahsisi oluşturup onaylar.
  • Yalnız içinde bulunulan hizmet yılı tahsis edilir; önceki yıllar elle girilir.
